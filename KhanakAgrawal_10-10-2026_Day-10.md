### APPROACH
The task is to check whether the last letter of the question is a vowel or not. First, spaces and punctuation marks are ignored by finding the last letter that is not a space. Then, the letter is converted to lowercase to handle both uppercase and lowercase inputs.

If the last letter is a vowel (`a`, `e`, `i`, `o`, `u`), the answer is `YES`; otherwise, it is `NO`.

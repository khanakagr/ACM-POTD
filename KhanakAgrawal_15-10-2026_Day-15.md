### APPROACH
The idea is to find the coordinates of the missing corner of a rectangle formed by four crime scenes on a grid.

A rectangle has four corners. Since three corners are already given, the missing corner can be found by checking the row and column coordinates: the row and column that appear only once among the three points identify the missing corner.

### APPROACH
The task is to convert the word to either all uppercase or all lowercase. First, count the number of uppercase and lowercase letters in the given string.

If the number of uppercase letters is greater than lowercase letters, convert the all the letters to uppercase. Otherwise, convert it into lowercase. Even if they are equal, convert to lowercase.

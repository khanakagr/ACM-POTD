### APPROACH
The task is to find the smallest rectangle containing all the `*` symbols in the given matrix. The minimum and maximum row and column indices where `*` appears are stored. These values give the boundaries of the rectangle.

Then finally, the characters within these boundaries are printed to get the required pattern.

### APPROACH
So my approach was, I stored all the heights in an array and then just checked the difference between every two people standing next to each other. Whenever I found a smaller difference, I stored that pair. And because they're standing in a circle, I also checked the last and first person separately. In the end, I just printed the pair with the smallest difference.

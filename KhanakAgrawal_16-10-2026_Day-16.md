### APPROACH
The task is to find how many pairs of non-negative integers a and b satisfy both the equations given in the question.
  
So, the solution is to try all possible values of a and b from 0 to 100. For each pair, check whether both equations are satisfied. 

If they are, increase the count and finally, print the total number of valid pairs.

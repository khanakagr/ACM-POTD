### APPROACH
The task is to check whether `n` is a triangular number or not. A number is triangular if it can be written as the sum of consecutive natural numbers starting from 1, like `1`, `3`, `6`, `10`, etc.

Starting from 1, numbers are added until the sum becomes equal to or greater than `n`. If the sum equals `n`, the answer is `YES`; otherwise, it is `NO`.
